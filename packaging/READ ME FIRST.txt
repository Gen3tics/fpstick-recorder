FPStick Recorder (beta)
=======================

Records a match of Overwatch on your PC and turns it into a page you can read
and share: https://fpstick.com

The guide with pictures: https://fpstick.com/guide


In this folder
--------------

  FPStick Recorder.exe       the program you start
  PresentMon-2.6.0-x64.exe   Intel's PresentMon, unchanged. It measures the
                             frames; the recorder starts it for you.
  licences                   the licences of both, and of the libraries used

Keep the two programs in the same folder. Nothing is installed: to remove the
recorder, delete this folder.


The short version
-----------------

1. Start Overwatch.

2. Double-click "FPStick Recorder".
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". It says so for every program
   that is new and not yet known to Microsoft. Choose "More info", then
   "Run anyway".

3. Press Record before your match. Windows asks whether the program may make
   changes to your PC: choose Yes. Measuring another program's frames needs
   administrator rights.

4. After five seconds you hear a sound. Play your match.

5. Hold your stop key for a second when the match is over. The stop key is
   shown on the first screen and can be changed there.

6. Look at what would be sent, then press "Send and open my run page".
   Nothing leaves your PC before you press it.

Your earlier runs are under the "Your runs" tab.


Where things are kept
---------------------

  Recordings:  the folder "Recorder" inside your Documents folder
  Settings:    %APPDATA%\Recorder\choices.json

What is sent and what is kept: https://fpstick.com/privacy
